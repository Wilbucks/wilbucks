# Wilbucks Web & Profile

Create a polished, responsive single-page website prototype for Marie Wilcox's personal/executive profile, intended to later be adapted into a page on her existing domain wilbucks.com. Do not claim it is published to wilbucks.com. Visual direction: editorial, confident, premium, minimal, credible executive/technology leader; light warm background, deep navy/charcoal typography, subtle cobalt accent, excellent typography and spacing, no generic stock cyber imagery, no gimmicky gradients. The page is an About Marie Wilcox page designed for SEO/AEO and conversion from recruiters, CEOs, journalists and conference organisers. Include: hero with H1 'Marie Wilcox — Go-to-Market, Cybersecurity & Technology Growth'; intro identifying her as a technology marketing and GTM executive, cybersecurity commentator and AI-enabled growth practitioner with 20+ years experience; CTAs 'Media & speaking enquiries' and 'Explore my experience'; sections for GTM and commercial growth; cybersecurity media commentary; AI-enabled marketing/GTM; professional leadership and credentials; featured media coverage; speaking topics; contact. Add a simple top navigation with About, Media & Press, GTM & Growth, Speaking, Contact (can be anchor links/sections on this prototype). SEO: title and meta description, semantic heading structure, accessible image alt text, canonical-ready metadata, Open Graph metadata, Person and ProfilePage JSON-LD structured data with only grounded info. Use a headshot placeholder area that can be replaced with a real photo, not a generated face. Press section should feature The Guardian and Sky News and note broader national/international and specialist cybersecurity coverage; avoid fabricating individual article links or dates beyond the October 2026 ASOS commentary. Career names: Binalyze, Panaseer, McLaren Applied, Digital Barriers, Siemens and BAE Systems. Claims to use carefully and not overstate: 20+ years' experience; go-to-market, product marketing, category creation, demand generation, analyst relations and communications; worked with Gartner on market categories; AI-enabled marketing workflows. Credentials: Fellow of Chartered Institute of Marketing (FCIM); professional leadership roles with CIM and CIISec, phrased conservatively as 'professional leadership and board roles' without implying current status if uncertain. Contact button should use mailto:marieawilcox@outlook.com and LinkedIn https://uk.linkedin.com/in/marieclutterbuck. No fabricated testimonials, metrics, press logos, schema facts, or endorsements. Include clear note in editor summary that this is a prototype and needs integration into the existing website; do not publish it to a new domain.
