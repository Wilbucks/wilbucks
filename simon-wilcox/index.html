Simon Wilcox.

Sales and commercial leadership.

Simon is a sales and commercial leader and a director of Wilbucks. His background includes technology and security, with a professional focus on data intelligence in security and government.

His areas of focus include business development, sales strategy, market entry and client relationships. He brings a commercial perspective to product strategy: how technical capability becomes a proposition a buyer can understand and assess.

Experience

From engineering to commercial leadership

Simon began his career in engineering and software roles within the UK Government, before moving into commercial technology and then into sales and business development. That technical grounding shapes how he works: he understands the capability behind a proposition, and how to present it so a buyer can assess its value.

Across a career of more than 25 years he has moved through software engineering, sales, business development and product strategy into senior commercial leadership in the government and national-security sector. At BAE Systems Detica he helped grow a sales team and the business around it, and at BAE Systems Applied Intelligence he led international business development, opening new markets and delivering consistent order growth.

At SecureCloud+ he shaped product strategy for secure cloud services, building pipeline and growing revenue. At QinetiQ's Naimuri he led data intelligence business development and grew the Homeland Security business. He now leads a government client group at Daintta.
