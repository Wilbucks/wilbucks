### About Wilbucks

**H1: About Wilbucks**

Wilbucks is an independent sales and marketing consultancy run by two directors, Marie Wilcox and Simon Wilcox. Marie's background is technology marketing. Simon's is sales and commercial leadership.

Our experience includes enterprise technology and cybersecurity. Those markets involve complex products, several people in a buying decision and scrutiny of the claims a supplier makes. We bring that experience to commercial questions across technology businesses.

**How we approach the work**

Before recommending a campaign, a new message or a different sales approach, we examine what is happening now. What are buyers saying? Where do opportunities stall? Does the proposition give sales enough to work with?

The task may be a specific positioning issue or a wider question about entering a market. We agree the scope and what the work needs to produce before proceeding.

**Working principles**

- **Use buyer feedback.** Test assumptions against sales conversations and available evidence.
- **Make decisions before adding activity.** More content or outreach is not always the answer.
- **Keep claims defensible.** Explain what the product can do without stretching the evidence.
- **Use tools for a reason.** AI is useful where it improves a defined task. Outputs still need checking.

**Actions:** Our directors · Contact Wilbucks

### Expertise

**H1: Sales and marketing expertise**

The questions below describe our areas of focus. The scope of an engagement is agreed separately.

**Commercial strategy and market entry**

Where is the strongest commercial opportunity? Examine target accounts, buying requirements and routes to market before committing sales and marketing effort. Define what entering a market would require, rather than treating interest as evidence of demand.

**Sales strategy and business development**

How are opportunities developed and pursued? Review account priorities, buyer conversations and the decisions that move a sale forward. Consider how client relationships support further business.

**Positioning and propositions**

Can a buyer explain why they would choose your product? Establish the relevant use case, the alternatives and the evidence behind the proposition. Use that in sales material and marketing messages.

**Product marketing and go-to-market**

What does sales need to take the product to market? Work through audiences, use cases and launch priorities. Make product information usable in a buying conversation, not just accurate as a technical description.

**Demand generation and pipeline**

Where does marketing interest stop becoming a sales opportunity? Examine targeting, channels, qualification and follow-up. Agree what counts as a useful opportunity and how sales and marketing assess it.

**AI-enabled marketing**

Which tasks are worth changing? Assess uses such as research, content preparation and workflow automation against information quality, time spent and review requirements. Do not adopt a tool simply because it uses AI.

Cybersecurity and enterprise technology provide context for this work. They are not separate claims of technical security services. These areas of focus do not imply current contracts or guaranteed pipeline or revenue results.
