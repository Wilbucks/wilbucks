### Home

**Wilbucks**  
*Clear thinking for technology, markets and growth.*

Strong technology needs a clear reason to buy. Wilbucks is an independent sales and marketing consultancy helping technology businesses explain their value, reach the right customers and decide where to focus their commercial effort.

We bring technology, cybersecurity and marketing perspectives to questions about positioning, routes to market and growth. The starting point is the business problem, not a list of activities.

**Actions:** Explore our expertise · Discuss your priorities

**Areas of focus**

- **Positioning and messaging:** Make it clear who your product is for, the problem it addresses and why it deserves consideration.
- **Go-to-market and demand:** Identify the buyers you need to reach and connect marketing activity with the sales conversations that matter.
- **Technology, cybersecurity and AI:** Explain complex issues in business terms and consider where AI can make marketing work more useful.

These areas guide an initial conversation. The scope of any work is agreed around your priorities.

**Why Wilbucks**

A product story, a marketing plan and a sales conversation should not pull in different directions. Wilbucks considers them together, looking at the technology, the buyer’s problem and the decisions needed to move forward. The directors bring different perspectives to that discussion, without adding unnecessary complexity.

### About Wilbucks

**About Wilbucks**  
*Sales and marketing thinking, grounded in technology.*

Wilbucks is an independent sales and marketing consultancy for technology businesses. Our focus is the connection between what a business builds, why customers should care and how sales and marketing support its commercial priorities.

The company brings together its two directors, Marie Wilcox and Simon Wilcox. Each has a distinct professional background. Their profiles set out that experience separately, rather than presenting one director’s career as the company’s history.

**Our approach**

Start with the product, the customer and the commercial question. Establish what needs to become clearer, who needs to be reached and what a useful next step would look like. Agree the scope before choosing activities or tools.

**Our principles**

- **Clarity before activity:** Know what needs to change before deciding what to do.
- **The buyer’s problem matters:** Explain value in terms customers recognise, not only technical features.
- **Sales and marketing belong together:** Connect the proposition, outreach and sales conversation.
- **Technology needs judgement:** Consider AI where it serves the task, with human responsibility for decisions and communication.
- **Claims need evidence:** Separate ambition from demonstrated results.

**Actions:** Meet our directors · Discuss a challenge
