01 / Career

Experience

Building and growing technology businesses

My career spans challenger businesses and established global organisations, including cybersecurity, enterprise technology and engineering. I've worked across the marketing function, from defining market positioning and creating demand to building teams, shaping corporate reputation and supporting commercial growth.

Across my career, I've created market categories recognised by Gartner, built enterprise pipeline, supported fundraising, acquisitions and business transformation, developed international marketing teams, and secured national and international media coverage.

I specialise in category creation, brand positioning, corporate communications, analyst relations, demand generation, AI-enabled marketing and developing high-performing international teams. I enjoy turning complex technologies into stories customers understand and sales teams can sell.
