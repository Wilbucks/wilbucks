About Wilbucks

Helping businesses turn great technology and services into growth.

We are a company set up to help SMEs and large organisations get the investment they need and really grow. Many organisations have great technology or services but communicate them poorly to their target markets, wasting money and resources and sometimes failing to succeed.

Our business is founded on more than 20 years of B2B experience across sales and marketing, working in large corporates and small start-ups, including businesses backed by private equity and venture capital from Series A to Series C. We help deliver better value propositions, and marketing and sales strategies that actually deliver revenue and growth. Wilbucks is led by two directors, Marie Wilcox and Simon Wilcox.

01 / Our approach

A proposition that meets a real customer need

Without a solid, clearly communicated value proposition, targeted at the right audience and meeting a real customer need, a business will struggle to achieve revenue or investment.

We help make sure your products and services are right for your customers, investors and markets by developing the right sales and marketing strategies to support growth, investment and ROI.

Where it adds real value, we also help businesses identify opportunities to embed AI into their systems and processes. The focus is practical: using AI to improve how work gets done, support better decisions and make teams more effective, with a clear purpose rather than technology for its own sake.

Who we work with

We work with UK B2B technology businesses, from founder-led start-ups to venture-backed and private equity-backed companies. Our experience is strongest in software, cybersecurity, AI, data and engineering, where a clear proposition and a joined-up sales and marketing plan make the biggest difference.
